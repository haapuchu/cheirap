## 2. Complete Scene-by-Scene Walkthrough Script (Preety's Narration)

### Scene 1: The Civic Dilemma (0:00 - 0:40)
* **Visual on Screen**: 
  * Brief opening title card showing CHEIRAP (`ꯆꯩꯔꯥꯞ`), Team Lotux, Manipur Technical University, Imphal.
  * Screen cuts to live browser view of `manipurtenders.gov.in`.
  * Quick scroll through dense tables, CAPTCHA forms, and 80-page legal PDF notices.
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"Every year, hundreds of crores of public funds are spent across Manipur through government tenders to build our roads, upgrade secondary schools, and equip district hospitals. But for everyday citizens, local MSMEs, and student researchers, understanding these notices is almost impossible.*
  >
  > *Information is locked inside bureaucratic legal dockets and dense scanned PDFs. Frequently, critical corrigenda are issued at the last minute, compressing bidding deadlines to narrow windows of under 72 hours. By the time citizens or local contractors find out, tenders are already closed. Moreover, the state portal offers zero access in Manipur's constitutional Meetei Mayek script. That is the civic gap our team set out to solve."*

---

### Scene 2: Introducing CHEIRAP & Citizen Explorer (0:40 - 1:25)
* **Visual on Screen**:
  * Smooth switch to CHEIRAP landing page (`http://localhost:5173`).
  * Highlight the Meetei Mayek branding (`ꯆꯩꯔꯥꯞ`) and the headline: *"See what public money is buying."*
  * Click **"Explore Tenders"**.
  * Type `Education` in search bar, select `Education Department`, and switch toggle to **Deadline View**.
  * Hover over status badges (*"Short bidding window"* in Amber, *"Unamended"* in Green).
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"Welcome to CHEIRAP. Named after Manipur's traditional court of public vigilance, CHEIRAP is a civic transparency layer that normalizes government tender data into plain-language briefs before decisions become irreversible.*
  >
  > *Our Citizen Explorer lets anyone search public tenders across departments, districts, and budget brackets. Instead of obscure tender reference codes, notices are clearly arranged by upcoming submission deadlines. Notice how CHEIRAP flags objective regulatory signals, such as compressed bidding windows or frequent corrigenda. We do not make legal accusations; we give citizens the objective context needed to ask informed questions."*

---

### Scene 3: The Tender Executive Brief (1:25 - 2:15)
* **Visual on Screen**:
  * Click on high-value tender `MAN_ED_PROC_2026_0142` (₹4.82 Cr Modular Labs).
  * Show the 4 Key Metrics cards: Estimated Value (₹4.82 Cr), Corrigenda (3), EMD (₹12.05 Lakh), Response Window (14.5 days).
  * Scroll through the **Plain-Language Decoder** (Who, What, Where, Budget).
  * Click the **"Verify on official portal"** external link button; show `manipurtenders.gov.in` opening in a new tab.
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"Let us examine a real tender: the 4.82 crore rupee procurement of modular labs for secondary schools in Manipur. Rather than wading through an 80-page docket, CHEIRAP instantly distills the essential details into four clear cards: estimated budget, total amendments, required earnest money deposit, and remaining bidding time.*
  >
  > *Our Plain-Language Decoder translates complex contractual clauses into four simple citizen questions: Who is buying? What is being procured? Where is it located? And what is the money breakdown?*
  >
  > *Crucially, every single data point is 100 percent verifiable. With one click, citizens can verify the source notice directly on the official state portal."*

---

### Scene 4: Visual Notice-Flow Stepper & Peer Comparison (2:15 - 3:00)
* **Visual on Screen**:
  * Click the **"Visual Timeline"** tab.
  * Mouse traces the continuous 4-stage stepper: *Notice Published -> Corrigenda -> Submission Deadline -> Technical Opening*.
  * Highlight the amber warning badge for compressed post-corrigendum window.
  * Click the **"Peer Comparison"** tab.
  * Show the comparative bar benchmark comparing this tender against historical Education Department tenders.
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"Under the Visual Timeline tab, CHEIRAP reconstructs the chronological history of the notice. Under Central Vigilance Commission guidelines and Rule 161 of the General Financial Rules, bidders must receive sufficient time to submit bids. When a late amendment compresses the remaining submission window to under 72 hours, CHEIRAP visually highlights this timeline compression.*
  >
  > *Under the Peer Comparison tab, we benchmark the tender against historical projects in the same department. Citizens can immediately observe whether a 14-day window is standard departmental practice or an anomalous outlier."*

---

### Scene 5: Citizen Action & Public Inquiry Drafter (3:00 - 3:30)
* **Visual on Screen**:
  * Click the **"Citizen Action"** tab.
  * Select *"Timeline inquiry"*.
  * Show the automatically generated formal inquiry draft with specific tender IDs, dates, and CVC references.
  * Click **"Copy source-cited inquiry"**; display the green confirmation toast.
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"Most importantly, CHEIRAP turns public awareness into constructive civic action. With our Citizen Inquiry Generator, any community leader, journalist, or resident can generate a formal clarification inquiry with a single click.*
  >
  > *The letter automatically cites the exact tender reference numbers, dates, and official clauses, making it ready for an RTI query or administrative clarification. Everything operates locally in the user's browser, ensuring absolute citizen privacy with zero tracking."*

---

### Scene 6: Conclusion & Viksit Bharat Impact (3:30 - 3:45)
* **Visual on Screen**:
  * Transition to the **Across India** national roadmap view.
  * Concluding slide showing Team Lotux, Manipur Technical University, and Hack for Social Cause banner.
* **Spoken Voiceover (Preety)**:
  > *"CHEIRAP is completely open source, fully tested, and built on an extensible adapter architecture ready to scale across all eight Northeastern states and national GePNIC portals.*
  >
  > *When citizens have clear, accessible public data, public governance becomes truly transparent. Thank you from Team Lotux!"*

### Delivery & Pronunciation Notes for Preety

* **CHEIRAP (ꯆꯩꯔꯥꯞ)**: Pronounced *"Chay-rup"* (with a soft 'p').
* **Meetei Mayek**: Pronounced *"May-tay My-yek"*.
* **GePNIC**: Pronounced *"Jep-nik"* (Government e-Procurement System of NIC).
* **GFR**: Pronounced *"G-F-R"* (General Financial Rules).
* **Pacing Tip**: Pause for half a second after each question in Scene 3 (*"Who is buying? What is being procured? Where is it located?"*) to let evaluators absorb the UI.
* **Smile & Projection**: Maintain eye contact with the camera during the intro, speaking clearly at a conversational, confident volume.
