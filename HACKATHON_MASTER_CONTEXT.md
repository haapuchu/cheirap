## 3. Trilateral Grand Jury Evaluation Framework (100% Total)

Every prototype undergoes rigorous evaluation across three distinct institutional dimensions:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        TRILATERAL JURY RUBRIC                          │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┤
│ TECHNICAL TRUST     │ GOVERNMENT RELEVANCE      │ INDUSTRY POTENTIAL   │
│       35%           │           30%             │         35%          │
├─────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ • Working Prototype │ • Dept Problem Fit        │ • Scalability & TAM  │
│ • Architecture      │ • Practical Value         │ • Product UX/UI      │
│ • AI Authenticity   │ • Government Feasibility  │ • Deployability      │
│ • Security/Privacy  │ • Real-World Adoption     │ • Pitch Presentation │
│ • Responsible AI    │ • Administrative Due-Proc │ • Jury Q&A Defense   │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

### 1. TECHNICAL TRUST (35% Weightage)
* **Core Question:** *Does the solution work, and can it be trusted by government auditors and engineers?*
* **Evaluation Criteria:**
  * **Working Prototype:** System runs reliably in real time with demonstrable inputs and outputs.
  * **Technical Architecture:** Sound, modular system design, clean API layers, and defensible technology choices.
  * **Effective Use of AI:** Meaningful application of machine learning, NLP, or computer vision beyond trivial API wrappers.
  * **Data Handling & Pipelines:** Robust data ingestion, clean validation, handling of missing data, and realistic test datasets.
  * **Security & Privacy:** Safeguards protecting citizen sensitive data and adhering to government data governance norms.
  * **Reliability & Responsible AI:** Explainable outcomes, deterministic boundary checks, and mitigation of algorithmic hallucinations.

### 2. GOVERNMENT RELEVANCE (30% Weightage)
* **Core Question:** *Does the solution genuinely solve a real departmental problem within Manipur's operational context?*
* **Evaluation Criteria:**
  * **Problem Comprehension:** Deep understanding of the operational pain points described in the problem statement.
  * **Departmental Fit:** Direct applicability to the specific working methods of the target department.
  * **Practical Usefulness:** Delivers tangible time, cost, or administrative efficiency gains to public servants or citizens.
  * **Citizen & Official Value:** Clear benefits for field workers, executive engineers, or citizen end-users.
  * **Government Feasibility:** Operable within existing government constraints (bandwidth limits, field conditions, legacy databases).
  * **Adoption Potential:** Ready pathway for pilot testing, sandbox integration, or departmental deployment.

### 3. INDUSTRY POTENTIAL (35% Weightage)
* **Core Question:** *Can the solution evolve into a sustainable, scalable enterprise product?*
* **Evaluation Criteria:**
  * **Problem–Solution Fit & Innovation:** Novel approach with a clear technological or workflow advantage over existing tools.
  * **Product Quality & UX:** Professional, responsive, and intuitive interface designed for non-technical officials and citizens.
  * **Scalability:** Architecture capable of scaling statewide across all districts and nationwide to other state governments.
  * **Deployment Viability:** Clear packaging, low maintenance overhead, and clear API integration interfaces.
  * **Pitch & Defense:** Persuasive, concise presentation and authoritative handling of challenging questions from the jury.

### 1. Social Welfare Department

#### SW-01 — AI-Based Welfare Scheme Eligibility & Assistance Navigator *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Citizens frequently do not know which state or central welfare schemes they qualify for, what documents are required, or how to initiate applications.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI-based system that understands a citizen's basic socio-economic profile and identifies eligible welfare schemes, required documentation, and application procedures. The system must provide accessible guidance while making clear that final eligibility determinations rest with the department.

#### SW-02 — AI-Based Welfare Grievance & Service Assistant *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Citizens struggle to navigate where to lodge grievances or seek verified information regarding old-age pensions, disability allowances, and family assistance.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI-powered multilingual assistant that comprehends citizen queries and grievances, pulls verified answers from an approved knowledge base, and guides citizens to the appropriate departmental grievance channel.

#### SW-03 — AI-Based Welfare Application Screening & Case Prioritisation *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Welfare officers face extensive backlogs manually auditing vast volumes of applications, identification proofs, and income certificates for inconsistencies.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI decision-support system that ingests application records, identifies missing or conflicting data, organizes cases by processing state, and surfaces anomalous or priority files requiring urgent departmental review.

#### SW-04 — AI-Based Welfare Service Gap & Coverage Analysis *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Administrators find it difficult to identify remote or vulnerable geographic clusters where eligible beneficiaries are underserved or where application processing is stalled.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI analytics platform that evaluates beneficiary and disbursement logs to expose geographic coverage gaps, delivery anomalies, application backlogs, and underserved areas requiring administrative intervention.

### 2. Agriculture Department

#### AG-01 — AI-Based Crop Disease Detection & Advisory *(Farmer-side)*
* **Farmer Problem:** Farmers face significant crop losses due to late identification of leaf pathogens, blight, and fungal infestations without accessible agronomic guidance.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI computer-vision system that analyzes crop and leaf photographs alongside environmental inputs to diagnose diseases, recommend preventive treatments, and route severe cases to agricultural extension officers.

#### AG-02 — AI-Based Agricultural Advisory Assistant *(Farmer-side)*
* **Farmer Problem:** Farmers lack continuous access to agronomists for timely advice on crop cycles, micro-climate weather events, soil preparation, and irrigation schedules.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI conversational assistant that interprets farmer inquiries and provides verified, easy-to-understand agronomic advisories sourced from an approved agricultural knowledge repository.

#### AG-03 — AI-Based Farmer Query & Field-Visit Prioritisation System *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** District agricultural officers receive unmanageable volumes of field assistance requests without an objective method to triage urgent visits.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI classification pipeline that categorizes incoming farmer queries by urgency and location, identifies emerging disease hotspots, and helps officers prioritize high-impact field visits.

#### AG-04 — AI-Based Crop & Pest Risk Monitoring System *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Proactive monitoring of pest vectors and crop stress across extensive hilly and valley terrain requires heavy field manpower.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI monitoring dashboard that fuses sample field reports, weather metrics, pest histories, and geographic data to predict regional crop risks and deliver an early-warning radar for agricultural directorates.

### 3. Public Works Department (PWD)

#### PWD-01 — AI-Based Road Damage Reporting & Detection *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Commuters encounter potholes, pavement failures, and landslides but struggle to report accurate geo-coordinates and defect severity to authorities.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI-enabled mobile/web interface allowing citizens to upload road photographs or video clips, automatically detecting pavement defects and capturing GPS coordinates for departmental reporting.

#### PWD-02 — AI-Based Citizen Road Complaint Assistant *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Citizens do not know which jurisdiction (National Highways, State PWD, Municipal Council, or Rural Roads) oversees specific road corridors.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI assistant that understands road complaints, determines the responsible jurisdictional division, and guides citizens to the correct reporting portal.

#### PWD-03 — AI-Based Road Inspection & Maintenance Prioritisation *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** PWD divisional engineers cannot continuously inspect every road corridor and must prioritize limited maintenance budgets based on structured necessity.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI prioritization model that analyzes road defect imagery, traffic volume, connectivity importance, and maintenance history to generate ranked maintenance schedules.

#### PWD-04 — AI-Based Construction Progress Monitoring *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Tracking multiple capital works projects across remote districts requires frequent field visits and continuous comparison of actual physical progress against planned milestones.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI system that analyzes periodic site photographs, videos, and project management metadata to verify visible physical progress, spot schedule slippages, and flag anomalies demanding official field inspection.

### 4. Transport Department

#### TR-01 — AI-Based Citizen Transport Service Assistant *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Navigating regional transport office (RTO) procedures, fee tables, driving licenses, and vehicle registration certificates is complex and opaque for citizens.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop a conversational AI assistant that delivers verified guidance on transport procedures, required documentation, and application workflows using official department knowledge bases.

#### TR-02 — AI-Based Road Safety Reporting & Risk Awareness System *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Citizens encounter dangerous road sections, hazardous turns, or recurring accident spots without an effective platform to report and track these risks.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI-enabled platform allowing citizens to log road safety hazards, analyzing incident clusters to create public hazard maps and awareness alerts.

#### TR-03 — AI-Based Transport Document Verification Assistant *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Transport officials manually verify thousands of commercial permits, fitness certificates, and insurance documents, causing verification backlogs.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI OCR and document analysis pipeline that parses transport documents, verifies completeness, and highlights tampered or anomalous filings for manual verification.

#### TR-04 — AI-Based Road Accident & Traffic Risk Analysis *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Authorities need to identify recurring crash blackspots and environmental risk factors to target engineering and enforcement interventions.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI system that analyzes accident history, traffic flow, road geometry, weather patterns, and geolocation data to detect risk patterns and generate predictive safety maps.

---

### 5. Education (S) Department

#### ED-01 — AI-Based Personalised Learning Assistant *(Student-side)*
* **Student Problem:** Students who struggle with specific concepts lack customized learning support tailored to their pace outside classroom hours.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an adaptive AI learning assistant that identifies individual conceptual gaps and provides personalized explanations, practice exercises, and learning modules suited to the student's level.

#### ED-02 — AI-Based Parent/Student School Information Assistant *(Citizen-side)*
* **Citizen Problem:** Parents and students struggle to access timely information regarding school facilities, academic calendars, attendance, and welfare entitlements.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop a conversational AI assistant that provides verified school information through an intuitive interface while strictly protecting student personal data.

#### ED-03 — AI-Based School Performance & Learning Gap Analytics *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Education directorates lack granular visibility across schools to detect systemic subject-wise learning deficits and identify underperforming institutions.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI analytics suite that analyzes assessment data to identify subject-wise and school-wise learning patterns, exposing institutional gaps requiring academic intervention.

#### ED-04 — AI-Based School Resource Planning System *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Authorities face imbalances in teacher postings, laboratory infrastructure, computer hardware, and classroom capacity across diverse school clusters.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI decision-support platform that evaluates school-level infrastructural and enrolment metrics to generate objective, evidence-based resource allocation plans.

---

### 6. Youth Affairs & Sports (YAS) Department

#### YAS-01 — AI-Based Sports Talent Identification *(Athlete-side)*
* **Athlete Problem:** Young athletes with high athletic potential in grassroots or rural communities are often missed by conventional scouting networks.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI assessment system that evaluates physical biometric measurements, fitness trial scores, and sport-specific parameters to highlight athletes meriting formal coach evaluations.

#### YAS-02 — AI-Based Athlete Performance & Technique Analysis *(Athlete-side)*
* **Athlete Problem:** Developing athletes lack continuous access to specialized sports biomechanics coaches to refine their physical technique and form.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop a computer-vision model that analyzes training videos to evaluate posture, joint angles, movement velocity, repetitions, and biomechanical form, delivering actionable coaching feedback.

#### YAS-03 — AI-Based Sports Facility Utilisation & Planning *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Sports directorates lack consolidated visibility into facility occupancy across stadiums and complexes, leading to scheduling bottlenecks and underutilized assets.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI system that evaluates facility usage, booking demand, and regional availability to predict utilization patterns and guide sports infrastructure planning.

#### YAS-04 — AI-Based Sports Event Scheduling & Management *(Government-side)*
* **Government Problem:** Organizing multi-sport state and district tournaments involves complex scheduling conflicts across athletes, match officials, and venues.
* **Problem Statement:** Develop an AI-assisted tournament management platform that automates registrations, builds conflict-free match schedules, flags venue overlaps, and compiles real-time scoreboards and event dossiers.
