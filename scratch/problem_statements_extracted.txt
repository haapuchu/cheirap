1. SOCIAL WELFARE DEPARTMENT
SW-01 — AI-Based Welfare Scheme Eligibility & Assistance Navigator
Citizen-side problem: Citizens may not know which government welfare schemes they 
are eligible for, what documents are required, or how to apply.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based system that understands a citizen's basic 
circumstances and identifies potentially relevant welfare schemes, eligibility 
requirements, required documents and application procedures. The system should 
provide simple, accessible guidance while clearly indicating that final eligibility is 
determined by the department.
SW-02 — AI-Based Welfare Grievance & Service Assistant
Citizen-side problem: Citizens may struggle to understand where to raise complaints 
or obtain information regarding pensions, assistance, disability services and other 
welfare programmes.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-powered multilingual assistant that understands 
citizen queries and grievances, identifies the relevant welfare service, provides 
information from an approved knowledge base and guides the citizen to the appropriate 
service or grievance channel.
SW-03 — AI-Based Welfare Application Screening & Case Prioritisation
Government-side problem: Officials may have to manually examine large numbers of 
applications, documents and case records, making it difficult to quickly identify 
incomplete or inconsistent applications.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based decision-support system that extracts 
information from applications and documents, identifies missing or inconsistent 
information, groups cases by status and highlights cases requiring official attention.
SW-04 — AI-Based Welfare Service Gap & Coverage Analysis
Government-side problem: Officials may find it difficult to identify areas where eligible 
beneficiaries are not being adequately reached or where applications and services are 
accumulating.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI analytics system that analyses beneficiary and 
service-delivery data to identify coverage gaps, unusual patterns, pending cases and 
geographical areas requiring greater administrative attention.

2. AGRICULTURE DEPARTMENT
AG-01 — AI-Based Crop Disease Detection & Advisory
Citizen/Farmer-side problem: Farmers may have difficulty identifying crop diseases at 
an early stage and deciding what action to take.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI system that analyses crop/leaf images and relevant 
information to identify possible diseases or abnormalities and provide basic preventive 
and management guidance, with referral to agricultural experts when necessary.
AG-02 — AI-Based Agricultural Advisory Assistant
Citizen/Farmer-side problem: Farmers may need timely advice on crops, cultivation 
practices, irrigation, pests and weather-related risks but may not have easy access to 
agricultural experts.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-powered farmer assistant that understands farmer 
queries and provides reliable, easy-to-understand advice using an approved agricultural 
knowledge base and available contextual information.
AG-03 — AI-Based Farmer Query & Field-Visit Prioritisation System
Government-side problem: Agricultural officers and extension workers may receive 
numerous farmer queries and requests for field assistance, making it difficult to 
prioritise cases and respond quickly.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI system that categorises farmer queries, identifies 
urgency and location, detects recurring issues and helps officials prioritise field visits 
and expert intervention.
AG-04 — AI-Based Crop & Pest Risk Monitoring System
Government-side problem: Agricultural officials need to monitor crop conditions and 
potential pest or disease outbreaks across large geographical areas, but field-based 
monitoring can be resource-intensive.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based monitoring system that combines sample 
crop, weather, pest and geographical data to identify areas showing increased risk and 
provide an early-warning dashboard for agricultural officials.

3. PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT
PWD-01 — AI-Based Road Damage Reporting & Detection
Citizen-side problem: Citizens encounter potholes, cracks and damaged roads but 
reporting these issues and providing accurate location information may not always be 
easy.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-enabled mobile/web system through which citizens 
can submit road photographs or videos, automatically identify visible road defects and 
capture their location for reporting to the concerned authority.
PWD-02 — AI-Based Citizen Road Complaint Assistant
Citizen-side problem: Citizens may not know which authority or service channel 
should handle different road-related complaints.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI assistant that understands citizen complaints, 
identifies the nature and location of the issue and guides the citizen towards the 
appropriate reporting or grievance channel.
PWD-03 — AI-Based Road Inspection & Maintenance Prioritisation
Government-side problem: PWD officials cannot physically inspect every road section 
frequently and must decide where limited maintenance resources should be deployed.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based system that analyses road images, road 
condition, traffic, connectivity, previous maintenance and other relevant data to identify 
defects and support maintenance prioritisation.
PWD-04 — AI-Based Construction Progress Monitoring
Government-side problem: Monitoring multiple infrastructure projects requires 
regular field inspections and comparison of actual progress with planned milestones.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI system that analyses periodic site 
photographs/videos and project information to identify visible progress, potential 
delays and areas requiring further inspection or intervention.

4. TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
TR-01 — AI-Based Citizen Transport Service Assistant
Citizen-side problem: Citizens may find it difficult to understand transport-related 
procedures, required documents, fees, services and application processes.
Problem Statement: Develop a conversational AI assistant that provides verified 
information from an approved government knowledge base and guides citizens through 
relevant transport services and procedures.
TR-02 — AI-Based Road Safety Reporting & Risk Awareness System
Citizen-side problem: Citizens may encounter dangerous road locations or recurring 
traffic-safety issues but may not have an effective way to report and understand such 
risks.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-enabled platform that allows citizens to report 
road-safety issues and uses location and incident information to identify recurring 
safety concerns.
TR-03 — AI-Based Transport Document Verification Assistant
Government-side problem: Transport officials may need to manually examine large 
volumes of vehicle and permit-related documents, making verification time-consuming.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based OCR and document-analysis system that 
extracts information, checks completeness and identifies inconsistencies or suspicious 
patterns for manual verification by officials.
TR-04 — AI-Based Road Accident & Traffic Risk Analysis
Government-side problem: Transport authorities need to identify locations and 
conditions associated with higher accident or traffic risk to support targeted 
interventions.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based system that analyses accident history, traffic 
volume, road characteristics, time, weather and location data to identify recurring risk 
patterns and generate a risk map.

5. EDUCATION (S) DEPARTMENT
ED-01 — AI-Based Personalised Learning Assistant
Citizen/Student-side problem: Students may struggle with particular subjects or 
concepts but may not receive learning support suited to their individual needs.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI learning assistant that identifies a student's 
learning gaps and provides personalised explanations, practice questions and learning 
activities appropriate to their level.
ED-02 — AI-Based Parent/Student School Information Assistant
Citizen-side problem: Parents and students may need information regarding school 
facilities, academic activities, attendance, programmes and other school-related 
services.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI assistant that provides verified school-related 
information through a simple conversational interface while ensuring that sensitive 
student information is appropriately protected.
ED-03 — AI-Based School Performance & Learning Gap Analytics
Government-side problem: Education officials need to analyse data from many 
schools to identify learning gaps and schools requiring additional academic support.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI analytics system that analyses 
anonymised/synthetic assessment data to identify subject-wise and school-wise 
learning patterns, gaps and areas requiring intervention.
ED-04 — AI-Based School Resource Planning System
Government-side problem: Education authorities need to plan teachers, classrooms, 
laboratories, computers and other resources across schools with different 
requirements.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based decision-support system that analyses 
school-level data and identifies resource gaps and priority areas to support evidence￾based planning.

6. YOUTH AFFAIRS & SPORTS DEPARTMENT
YAS-01 — AI-Based Sports Talent Identification
Citizen/Athlete-side problem: Young athletes with potential may not always get 
opportunities to be identified through conventional selection processes.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based system that analyses fitness measurements, 
performance records and sport-specific parameters to identify athletes who may merit 
further assessment by qualified coaches.
YAS-02 — AI-Based Athlete Performance & Technique Analysis
Citizen/Athlete-side problem: Athletes may have difficulty objectively analysing their 
technique and performance without continuous access to specialised coaching.
Problem Statement: Develop a computer-vision system that analyses athlete videos to 
identify movement patterns, posture, repetitions, speed or other relevant performance 
indicators and provides useful feedback for coaches and athletes.
YAS-03 — AI-Based Sports Facility Utilisation & Planning
Government-side problem: Sports authorities may not have a consolidated view of 
how frequently facilities are used, when demand is highest and which facilities are 
underutilised.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-based system that analyses facility usage, location, 
demand and availability to predict utilisation patterns and support better scheduling 
and infrastructure planning.
YAS-04 — AI-Based Sports Event Scheduling & Management
Government-side problem: Organising sporting events involves managing athletes, 
teams, venues, matches and schedules while avoiding multiple conflicts.
Problem Statement: Develop an AI-assisted system that manages registrations, 
generates schedules, detects venue/team conflicts and provides event and 
performance reports.
