==================== PAGE 3 ====================
Guidelines
Every team will go through three rounds of evaluation:
1. TECHNICAL TRUST — 35%
Focus: Does the solution work, and can it be trusted?
Judges will assess:
Working prototype
Technical architecture and implementation
Effective use of AI
Data handling
Security and privacy
Reliability and responsible AI practices
2. GOVERNMENT RELEVANCE — 30%
Focus: Does the solution genuinely address a government
problem?
Judges will assess:
Understanding of the problem statement
Relevance to the concerned department
Practical usefulness
Citizen/official value
Feasibility within a government context
Potential for adoption or further testing
3. INDUSTRY POTENTIAL — 35%
Focus: Can the solution evolve into a practical, scalable product?
Judges will assess:
Problem–solution fit
Innovation
Product quality and user experience
Scalability
Deployment potential
Presentation and ability to respond to questions
